How information is verified

  • Events come only from official listings: HKCEC event calendar, AsiaWorld-Expo, InvestHK (HKSAR Government) and chamber of commerce calendars (HKGCC, AmCham, BritCham). Dates, venue and access type (Trade / Public / By registration / By invitation) are copied from the listing. If the listing does not state something, it shows "Not stated".
  • Property movements come from news headlines (Google News Hong Kong editions in English & Chinese, SCMP Property, Mingtiandi) and HKSAR Government press releases. Headlines are shown exactly as published with a link. Company, building, area, size and floors are filled in only when they appear in the text.
  • Reports of the same deal from different outlets, in either language, are grouped. Verification shows "Official source", "Corroborated by N outlets" or "Single outlet: open source to confirm".
  • Opportunity rating and angle are rule-based analysis, not reported fact. Each record lists the reasons that produced its score.
  • Items outside Hong Kong, market commentary, opinion pieces and people moves are filtered out. Concerts and exams are excluded from events.
