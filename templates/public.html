    Verification

    • Events come only from official listings: the HKCEC event calendar, AsiaWorld-Expo, InvestHK (HKSAR Government), HKGCC, AmCham and BritCham. Dates, venue, access type (Trade / Public / By registration / By invitation) and organiser phone/email are copied from the listing.
    • Office move-ins come from news headlines in Google News Hong Kong editions (English and Chinese), searched across Hong Kong business outlets. These include SCMP, The Standard, The Real Deal, Mingtiandi, Hong Kong Business, Law.com, Bloomberg, Reuters, RTHK, HKFP, The Young Reporter, HKET, HKEJ, Sing Tao, HK01, on.cc and others. Headlines are shown as published, with links.
    • Only office moves into Hong Kong are kept: new HQs, relocations, leases, expansions and new offices. Shop leases, closures, move-outs, land sales, residential deals and market commentary are removed, as are items without a named company.
    • Company, building, size and floors appear only when stated in the source. Reports of the same move are grouped across outlets and languages and labelled "Corroborated by N outlets" or "Single outlet: open source to confirm".
    • Priority, why go, who to meet, pitch and action plan are sales guidance built from rules and Securitas HK's service range: guarding, mobile patrol, electronic security, remote viewing, Pinkerton risk services and MySecuritas. They are not reported facts.
